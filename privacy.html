Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This page explains what information we collect when you join the 5-Day Python Automation Engine Sprint, what we do with it, and the choices you have. The challenge is run by us at ResilianceLab.Africa.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your first name and email address. That is all we ask for. If you reply to one of our emails, we also receive whatever you choose to write in your reply.

How we use it

  • To send you the five daily emails in the challenge, including the downloads and templates.
  • To answer your replies and questions.
  • To tell you about our paid Premium Automation Toolkit. The final email of the challenge promotes it, and we may email you about it and related products afterwards. You can opt out at any time.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who handles your data

We use a few services to run the challenge. They process your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Netlify hosts this website and stores form submissions.
  • Netlify forms stores your name and email and sends our emails.
  • Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS CDN load the fonts and styles for this site. Your browser requests these files directly, so those providers may see your IP address.
  • Trello, if you choose to use it for Day 1, is a separate service with its own privacy policy. We do not receive your Trello data.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own.

Cookies and tracking

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not set cookies ourselves. If this changes, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you stay subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we remove you from our mailing list. We may keep a minimal record that you unsubscribed so we do not email you again.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your local data protection laws. Contact us and we will do our best to help.

Children

This challenge is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
